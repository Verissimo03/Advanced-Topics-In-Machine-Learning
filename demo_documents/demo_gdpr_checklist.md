# Demo GDPR Checklist for SMEs

## Purpose

This checklist helps a small company prepare basic GDPR documentation before asking for professional legal review.

## Records of Processing

The company should keep a simple record of:

- Categories of personal data collected.
- Purpose of each processing activity.
- Legal basis for each processing activity.
- Categories of data subjects.
- Internal systems where personal data is stored.
- Retention period for each data category.

## Privacy Notice

The company should prepare a privacy notice explaining:

- Who controls the data.
- What personal data is collected.
- Why the data is used.
- The legal basis for processing.
- How long the data is kept.
- Who receives the data.
- How individuals can exercise their rights.

## Data Subject Rights

The company should have an internal process for access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, and portability requests.

## Processor Agreements

If the company uses service providers that process personal data, it should check whether a data processing agreement is needed. Examples include payroll providers, CRM systems, cloud storage providers, email marketing tools, and IT support companies.

## Security Measures

The company should document reasonable security measures such as access controls, strong passwords, backups, device protection, and staff training.

## Breach Response

The company should have a process to identify, investigate, document, and escalate possible personal data breaches.

## Human Review Trigger

Professional legal review is recommended if the company processes sensitive data, transfers data outside the EU/EEA, has a possible data breach, uses large-scale monitoring, or is unsure about its legal basis.

## Demo Notes

This is a simplified fictional checklist for classroom demonstration only. It is not legal advice.
