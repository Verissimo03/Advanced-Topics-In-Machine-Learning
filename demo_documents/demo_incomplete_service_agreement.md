# Demo Incomplete Service Agreement

## Parties

This service agreement is between Nova Web Studio Lda. ("Provider") and GreenMarket Retail Lda. ("Client").

## Services

The Provider will design and maintain a basic e-commerce website for the Client. The Provider will also provide monthly technical support.

## Fees

The Client will pay EUR 1,200 for the initial website setup and EUR 250 per month for support.

## Timeline

The Provider expects to deliver the first version of the website within six weeks after receiving content from the Client.

## Client Responsibilities

The Client must provide product descriptions, images, prices, and access to its domain provider.

## Confidentiality

The Provider must keep business information received from the Client confidential.

## Missing or Weak Clauses for Demo Purposes

This draft intentionally does not include:

- A clear termination clause.
- A liability cap.
- A GDPR/data processing clause.
- A governing law and jurisdiction clause.
- A service level or support response time.
- A process for change requests.
- Ownership terms for website code and design assets.

## Demo Notes

This is a fictional incomplete agreement for classroom demonstration only. It is not legal advice and should not be used as a real contract.
