# Demo Supplier Agreement

## Parties

This supplier agreement is between Atlantic Office Supplies Lda. ("Supplier") and BrightDesk Consulting Lda. ("Client"). The Supplier will provide office equipment and monthly maintenance services to the Client in Lisbon, Portugal.

## Scope of Services

The Supplier will deliver desks, chairs, monitors, and replacement accessories requested by the Client. Maintenance requests should be answered within five business days.

## Payment Terms

The Client will pay each invoice within 45 days of receipt. The agreement does not define late payment interest, invoice dispute procedures, or a maximum monthly spend.

## Term and Termination

The agreement starts on 1 September 2026 and renews automatically every year. Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice. The Client may terminate immediately if the Supplier repeatedly fails to deliver agreed equipment.

## Confidentiality

Both parties must keep commercial information, pricing, and internal business information confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

## Liability

The Supplier is liable for direct damages caused by failure to deliver the agreed equipment. The agreement does not include a clear liability cap. It also does not explain whether indirect damages, lost profits, or service interruption losses are excluded.

## Data Protection

The Supplier may receive names, business email addresses, delivery addresses, and phone numbers of Client employees. The agreement does not include a data processing agreement or detailed GDPR processor obligations.

## Governing Law

This agreement is governed by Portuguese law. Disputes should first be discussed between the parties in good faith.

## Demo Notes

This is a fictional document for classroom demonstration only. It is not legal advice and should not be used as a real contract.
